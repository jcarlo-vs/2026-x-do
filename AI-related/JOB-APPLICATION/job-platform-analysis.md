## 🇵🇭 1. OnlineJobs.ph — THE Filipino Platform (Why I Should Have Included This First!)

### What It Is
The biggest remote job platform **specifically built for Filipino workers**. US, Australian, and UK employers post jobs here specifically because they want to hire Filipinos. This is your home turf.

### Why Your Odds Are Highest Here
- Employers come here **specifically looking for Filipinos** — no competing against Indians, Eastern Europeans, etc.
- Full stack devs with your stack are in very high demand here
- Employers already understand Philippine time zones and culture
- No bidding wars — you apply directly like a job application
- Rates are posted upfront — no guessing games

### How It Works
- Employers post jobs with salary/rate listed
- You apply directly with your resume and portfolio link
- No points, no credits, no bidding system
- Simple and straightforward

### What You Can Expect to Earn
| Role | Monthly Rate |
|---|---|
| Full Stack Developer (Mid) | $800 - $1,500/mo |
| Full Stack Developer (Senior) | $1,500 - $3,000/mo |
| Next.js + AWS Dev | $2,000 - $4,000/mo |
| AI-integrated Dev | $2,500 - $5,000/mo |

> 💡 These are **full-time** monthly rates. Part-time and hourly contracts also exist.

### Pros
- ✅ 0% commission — you keep everything
- ✅ Built for Filipinos — employers expect and prefer PH applicants
- ✅ No complex system to learn — just apply
- ✅ Long-term full-time contracts common (stable income)
- ✅ Employers already know PH salary expectations

### Cons
- ❌ Rates are lower than Western platforms (but stable and consistent)
- ❌ Requires a paid membership (~$69/year) to apply to jobs — worth every peso
- ❌ Some employers lowball — know your worth and negotiate

### How to Stand Out
- Complete profile with portfolio link
- Add GitHub with active repos
- Mention AWS and AI integration prominently — rare skill here
- Apply to 5-10 jobs per day consistently

## 📋 2. Upwork — Biggest Platform, But Has a Learning Curve

### What It Is
The world's largest freelancing platform with millions of jobs posted. Very established, very competitive — but also very rewarding once you understand how it works.

### ⚠️ Understanding Upwork "Connects" (The Point System)

This is what confuses most beginners. Here's exactly how it works:

**Connects are Upwork's virtual currency for applying to jobs.**

| Action | Connects Used |
|---|---|
| Apply to a fixed-price job | 6 Connects |
| Apply to an hourly job | 6 Connects |
| Boost your proposal (optional) | Extra connects |
| Apply to featured jobs | More connects |

**How You Get Connects:**
- New accounts get **80 free Connects** when you sign up
- After that you buy them: **$0.15 per Connect** (~$0.90 per job application)
- You also earn free Connects monthly based on your account level
- Unused Connects **roll over** (up to 200 max)

> 💡 **Reality check:** 80 free connects = about 13 job applications. Use them wisely on jobs that match your skills perfectly.

**Connects Tips:**
- Don't waste Connects on jobs with 50+ applicants early on
- Target jobs posted **within the last few hours** — fresher = less competition
- Look for jobs from clients with **verified payment method** (blue badge)
- Jobs with "Invite Only" label = client chose you = free to apply

### How Upwork Fees Work
| Earnings with Client | Upwork's Cut | Your Cut |
|---|---|---|
| First $500 | 20% | 80% |
| $500.01 - $10,000 | 10% | 90% |
| Over $10,000 | 5% | 95% |

> 💡 Once you hit $10k with a single client, the fee drops massively. Long-term contracts are gold on Upwork.

### Your Odds on Upwork
- **High** — if you niche down to Next.js + AI or AWS specifically
- **Medium** — if you apply generically as "full stack developer"
- The key is a **killer profile** and a **customized proposal** every time

### Tips for Getting Your First Upwork Client
1. Set your rate lower initially ($20-25/hr) — just to get first review
2. Only apply to jobs where you match **90%+ of requirements**
3. Write proposals that address the client's specific problem — not a generic pitch
4. Include a relevant portfolio link or GitHub in every proposal
5. Respond fast — clients often hire whoever replies first and best

### Pros
- ✅ Massive job volume — hundreds of Next.js/Node jobs daily
- ✅ Payment protection — you always get paid
- ✅ Once you have reviews, clients come to you
- ✅ Long-term contracts available (stable income)
- ✅ Very established Filipino developer community

### Cons
- ❌ Connects cost money — budget ~$10-20/month for applications
- ❌ Very competitive at Entry level (no reviews yet)
- ❌ 20% fee on first $500 with each new client (ouch)
- ❌ Takes time to build profile reputation

## 🆓 3. Contra — Best Zero-Fee Platform

### What It Is
A newer freelance platform with **zero commission** — you keep 100% of what you earn. Growing fast and gaining traction with US startups.

### Why It's Great for You
- No Connects, no credits, no fees
- Clean modern platform that startups love
- Your AI + Next.js skills are perfect for the startup crowd here
- Less saturated than Upwork — less competition

### How It Works
- Build a portfolio profile
- Get matched by the platform OR apply directly to posted projects
- Invoice through Contra — they handle payment processing
- You receive 100% of the agreed amount

### What You Can Earn
- Hourly: $30 - $80/hr depending on your positioning
- Project-based: $500 - $5,000+ per project

### Pros
- ✅ 0% commission — biggest advantage
- ✅ Less competition than Upwork
- ✅ Clean, modern platform — easy to use
- ✅ No confusing point systems

### Cons
- ❌ Smaller client pool than Upwork
- ❌ Newer platform — fewer long-term opportunities
- ❌ Less payment protection than Upwork

## 💼 4. LinkedIn — Highest Quality Clients, Slowest Start

### What It Is
Not a traditional job board — it's a professional network. But remote freelance and contract opportunities are everywhere here if your profile is optimized.

### Why It Works for Your Stack
- Recruiters actively search for "Next.js developer" or "AWS developer" daily
- Direct company contracts = higher rates, longer engagements, no middleman
- AI + cloud skills make your profile rank in recruiter searches
- You can reach out directly to hiring managers

### How to Get Hired on LinkedIn
1. **Optimize your headline** — "Full Stack Developer | Next.js • AWS • AI Integration | Open to Remote Contracts"
2. **Turn on Open to Work** — set it to "Contract" and "Freelance"
3. **Post content** — share what you build, even small things. Visibility = opportunities
4. **Connect with US/AU recruiters** — send a short, professional message
5. **Apply to job postings** — filter by "Remote" and "Contract"

### Pros
- ✅ 0% fee — direct client relationship
- ✅ Higher quality clients (established companies)
- ✅ Inbound opportunities once your profile is strong
- ✅ Builds your personal brand long-term

### Cons
- ❌ Slowest to generate income — takes weeks to months
- ❌ Requires consistent effort (posting, connecting, messaging)
- ❌ Not a traditional job board — learning curve on strategy

## ✅ 5. Arc.dev — Best Vetted Platform for Your Stack

### What It Is
A vetted talent network that matches senior developers with US companies. Known specifically for React, Next.js, and Node.js talent.

### Vetting Process
1. Submit application
2. Technical English screening
3. Live technical interview (your stack)
4. If accepted → matched with clients or listed in marketplace

### What You Can Earn
- $40 - $100/hr for contract work
- $60K - $130K/year for full-time remote

### Pros
- ✅ Clients are pre-qualified — they're serious and have budget
- ✅ Your exact stack (Next.js, Node, AWS) is what they want
- ✅ Low/no fee model — they charge the client
- ✅ Once accepted, inbound opportunities

### Cons
- ❌ Vetting takes 1-2 weeks
- ❌ Need portfolio to get through screening
- ❌ Rejection = no second chance for 6 months
