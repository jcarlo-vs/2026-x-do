## 🏆 Tier 1 — Premium / Vetted Platforms (Highest Pay)

### 1. [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com)
- **Type:** Freelance (Contract)
- **Rate:** $60 - $200+/hr
- **Cut:** 0% after acceptance (Toptal charges the client)
- **Process:** Rigorous screening (top 3%) — English test, logic, technical, live coding
- **Best for:** Senior devs who want premium US/EU clients
- **🇵🇭 Filipino-friendly:** Yes, many Filipinos are on Toptal

### 2. [Arc.dev](https://www.arc.dev)
- **Type:** Freelance + Full-time Remote
- **Rate:** $40 - $100/hr
- **Cut:** Small fee on client side, you keep most
- **Process:** Vetted, takes 1-2 weeks
- **Best for:** React/Next.js devs — your stack is perfect here

### 3. [Lemon.io](https://www.lemon.io)
- **Type:** Freelance
- **Rate:** $40 - $90/hr
- **Cut:** They charge the client, not you
- **Process:** Technical interview + code review
- **Best for:** Frontend-heavy devs (React, Vue, Next.js)

### 4. [Gun.io](https://www.gun.io)
- **Type:** Freelance Contract
- **Rate:** $50 - $150/hr
- **Cut:** Minimal
- **Process:** Vetted platform, US clients only
- **Best for:** Node.js + AWS devs

## 🥈 Tier 2 — High Volume + Good Pay

### 5. [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com)
- **Type:** Freelance
- **Rate:** You set it — aim for $30-80/hr
- **Cut:** 10% after $500 earned with a client (used to be 20%)
- **Process:** Create profile, pass skill tests, start bidding
- **Best for:** Building reviews fast, then raising rates
- **🇵🇭 Tip:** Many Filipinos succeed here — niche down to AI + Next.js

### 6. [Contra](https://www.contra.com)
- **Type:** Freelance
- **Rate:** You set it
- **Cut:** **0% commission** — you keep everything 🎉
- **Process:** Build profile, get matched or apply directly
- **Best for:** Starting out — zero fees is huge

### 7. [Fiverr Pro](https://www.fiverr.com/pro)
- **Type:** Freelance (Project-based)
- **Rate:** $500 - $5,000+ per project
- **Cut:** 20% (ouch, but volume is massive)
- **Process:** Apply for Pro badge to get premium clients
- **Best for:** Productized services (e.g. "I'll build your Next.js app")

### 8. [PeoplePerHour](https://www.peopleperhour.com)
- **Type:** Freelance
- **Rate:** $25 - $80/hr
- **Cut:** 20% up to £500, then drops
- **Best for:** UK and EU clients

## 🇵🇭 Philippines-Specific Tips

- **Payment:** Use **Wise** or **Payoneer** — lowest fees for receiving USD
- **Tax:** Register as a freelancer with BIR (8% flat tax option if under ₱3M/year)
- **Rate Negotiation:** Always quote in USD, never PHP — anchors you to international rates
- **Time Zone:** Philippine time works great for AU/NZ clients and async US roles
- **Portfolio:** Host on Vercel (free), link your GitHub — both are expected
